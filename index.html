07 — FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Ask it on the audit call — no pitch, no pressure.

What kind of tasks can you automate?+

If your team does it every week, it can probably be automated: lead follow-up, customer questions, appointment booking, client onboarding, invoicing, email management, CRM updates, social and content workflows, and hand-offs between apps.

Do I need to change the tools I use?+

Almost never. I build on top of what you already use — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, your CRM or database — so your team doesn't have to learn anything new.

How much does it cost?+

Every project is fixed-price, agreed before any work starts, and based on scope. The audit is free, and you'll know the exact cost — and the time and money it should save you — before you commit.

Is my data safe?+

Yes. Automations run inside your own accounts, I use least-privilege access, and I'm used to working under strict compliance requirements in a regulated industry. Happy to sign an NDA.

What happens if something breaks?+

Every build includes error alerts and 30 days of support. After that, you can fix small things using the docs I hand over, or move to a monthly partner plan.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?+

Yes — I already work remotely with a US-based team, and I schedule calls to overlap with US, UK, Gulf and European hours.
