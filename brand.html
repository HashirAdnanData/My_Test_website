●  Brand guidelines · 2026

Build the system once. Let it run.

The visual language of Hashir Adnan — AI Automation. Calm, precise and engineered. Everything is borrowed from the flow diagram: nodes, connectors and a signal that moves through them.

Personality

Calm, precise, quietly confident. An engineer who explains in plain English.

Visual motif

Nodes and connectors. One lime pulse = work happening automatically.

Golden rule

Lime is the only loud color. Use it on ~5% of any screen, never more.

05 — Voice

Say it like an engineer would

✓ Do

  • "Every lead gets a reply in under a minute."
  • "Cut manual effort by 20% for a US-based team."
  • "Fixed scope. Fixed price. Live in 2–3 weeks."

✕ Don't

  • "Revolutionize your business with cutting-edge AI synergy."
  • "10x your productivity overnight!!"
  • Jargon without an outcome attached.
